Content-derived transition

The desktop composition stays intact through 680px. At 679px it becomes constrained and the sidebar is replaced by the modal drawer. This boundary is derived from the selected shell rather than from a generic device category.

Constrained modal drawer behavior:
  • Initial focus is on Close; focus remains contained in the modal and returns to the menu trigger on dismissal.
  • Close, Escape, scrim activation, or Status selection dismisses the drawer. No swipe gesture is required.
  • The background is inert and scroll-locked while open; the drawer list remains independently scrollable.
  • Reduced motion removes the short drawer and scrim transition.
