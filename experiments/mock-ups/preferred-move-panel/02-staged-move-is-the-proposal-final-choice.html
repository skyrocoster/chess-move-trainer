The staged move is the proposal.

This sheet isolates the selected direction. Keep two facts in view: the current saved choice and the move staged now. When the staged move differs, it is also the proposal that Save would make current.

Design thesis Do not repeat the staged move in a third box. Give the right-hand box a clear consequence sentence instead. Current saved choice ↔ Staged move / proposal
2

Two boxes, one honest mutation. The left box is persisted now. The right box is staged and not persisted until Save; if it differs, saving makes it the current choice and replaces the old one.

What the right box means

Chosen model

When the staged move differs and the state is saveable, it carries two truths at once: it is the move currently staged, and it is the proposal that Save would persist.

Current saved choiceThe move the record uses now; its effective date belongs here.
Staged moveTemporary board choice; the consequence sentence says whether it can become current.

Action hierarchy

Three labels

Save leads when a staged difference can become the saved choice. Change effective date stays secondary. Remove is destructive but visually quiet.
